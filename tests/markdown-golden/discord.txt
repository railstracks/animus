# Release Notes 0.4.8

The **federation epoch** store landed, and *envelopes* are end-to-end encrypted with `sealed payloads`.

## Highlights

- Epoch store with per-node envelopes
- X25519 to HKDF to AES-GCM chain
- Loud lockout on boot unwrap failure
